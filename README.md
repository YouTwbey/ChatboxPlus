###### A basic chatbox mod for OSCLoader that displays basic info about your device, any media currently being played (only on PC) and info about what is happening in-game along with a functioning player list.

This mod is mainly a very basic showcase of OSCLoader's capabilities, supporting both PC and Standalone Quest.

# Chatbox Content
## Device Info
This chatbox mod displays the client's local time, their device's battery percent and their RAM usage.
## Media (PC Only)
When on PC, it will also display info about a client's currently ongoing media, whether that be from YouTube, Spotify, or anything else.
## In-Game events
Events such as players joining, stickers being placed, objects being picked up by the client, etc will be displayed when they happen. This is mainly just to showcase OSCLoader's capability of reading these events. Special events for worlds such as Prison Escape, Blackout and more will have special unique messages such as when the client dies or a role/team they are assigned to when a game starts.
## Player List
This chatbox mod also displays a functioning player list, showing the world name, how many players are currently in the instance along with the names of everyone who is currently inside the instance.
